# Limitations

---

## What did these models struggle with?

- All models focused on eye borders and edge artifacts, to varying degrees.
- There was a clear inverse performance-severity relationship where all models achieved excellent results on healthy cases but worse, sometimes very inaccurate results on severe cases.
- The transfer learning approach, while computationally efficient, may have introduced biases from natural image pretraining that don't completely translate to medical imaging tasks.
